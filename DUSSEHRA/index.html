THE MEANING BEHIND THE FESTIVAL

A Story of Good Over Evil

Dussehra, also known as Vijayadashami, celebrates the victory of good over evil. It is associated with Lord Rama's victory over Ravana and, in many traditions, Goddess Durga's victory over Mahishasura.
